## Visual notes

- The primary wordmark uses blue and gray with a stylized truck/road symbol.
- `profile-01.jpg` is suitable for a strong logistics hero because it shows a large closed-box truck on a road with high contrast.
- `service-01.jpg` shows warehouse handling with a forklift and palletized cargo; it is suitable for an operational service section.
- Fleet images are relatively small and should be used as contained catalog images, not stretched as full-width hero backgrounds.
